Two-panel reliance timeline, 2000–2025. Updated 17 October 2026.
The 250-jurisdiction inventory and all 6,500 historical category/date cells are unchanged.
Adopted provisions continue unless an explicit end is recorded, as requested by the user.
Only unresolved reference lists are filled from current or later publicly documented rosters. Known dated complete lists are retained. A dagger marks retrospective assignment, which is a descriptive assumption, not verified historical membership. This is not an estimate of exact historical network changes.
Numbers count distinct foreign national regulatory systems and separately named regional/global bodies. Multiple German agencies are grouped into the German national system; EMA is separate from national EU regulators. Explicit jurisdiction groups may be expanded where their member list is supplied; an unexpanded restricted group leaves a starred lower bound. Scope-specific evidence, pilots and frameworks retain their category; none becomes universal marketing authorisation.
NS = a named list is not specified in the reviewed public evidence. An asterisk means the displayed count is a known subset. n = explicitly any foreign authority, subject to the recorded national discretion. NS and unknown history never mean zero or confirmed absence. Canada’s any-foreign-review rule can be switched out of the reach plot; it is foreign evidence at discretion, not automatic acceptance.
Each reach point counts distinct destination jurisdictions per reference system. All recorded scopes are included. This includes limited product, quality-evidence, WHO, framework and pilot scopes; the chart is not a count of unrestricted new-drug fast tracks. Source/profile details remain in cell and line-point details. It measures potential eligibility, not pathway use or observed approvals. Data do not establish absence in grey cells.
No current membership is used to backdate a country’s initial adoption. Announcement dates and drafts do not create adopted routes. The Malawi 2026 second draft is not treated as a final roster; Georgia’s paid current consolidation was not downloaded.
